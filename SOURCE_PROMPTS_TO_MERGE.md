## Merge rules
- Extract all requirements, including minor UI details, text labels, animations, interactions, responsive behavior, integrations, constraints, and deployment details.
- Deduplicate repeated requirements without removing extra detail.
- If instructions conflict, preserve both in a conflict list and resolve based on explicit user intent, not guesswork.
- Do not treat a blank placeholder or a link alone as the contents of a conversation.
- After merging, write the consolidated, traceable requirements to `PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` and list unresolved conflicts or questions.
